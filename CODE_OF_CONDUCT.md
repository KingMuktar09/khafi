# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone who takes part in this project should feel welcome and safe. We are all here to learn and to build a useful library.

## Good behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Use simple, respectful words.
- Welcome new people. Help them with their first contribution.
- Accept feedback with grace. Say thank you when someone helps you.
- Talk about the work, not the person.

## Not allowed

- Rude, insulting, or threatening words.
- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing someone's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic messages.

## If something goes wrong

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainer. You can open a private report through GitHub, or contact the maintainer through the repo. The maintainer will look at it and may warn the person, hide a comment, or block them from the project.

This is a small project run by one maintainer. Decisions about conduct are made by the maintainer, and the goal is always to keep the project welcoming and useful.
